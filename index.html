Make A Mug Brownie! 😃

How to make mug brownies -->

Ingredients:

  • 3 Tbsp Flour
  • 3 Tbsp Cocoa Powder
  • 3 Tbsp Sugar
  • Pinch Salt
  • 3 Tbsp Milk
  • 2 1/2 Tbsp Oil
  • Handful of Chocolate Chips

Instructions:

  1. Combine flour, cocoa powder, sugar, and salt
  2. Whisk in oil and milk
  3. Fold in chocolate chips

    4. NOTE:

    Don't overmix, your treat will become dense!

  4. Microwave for 1 minute

    5. OPTIONAL: Place a scoop of ice cream on top

  5. Sprinkle some more chocolate chips on top
  6. ENJOY!

Pro Tips For This Recipe

  • Make sure to measure your flour correctly.
  • I highly recommend always using a scale to measure.
  • A shorter, wider mug is ideal as it allows for more even cooking.
  • Avoid overcooking your brownie in a cup, as you will end up with a dry brownie.
  • The center will continue to bake and set after you remove the mug from the microwave, so don't worry if they seem a bit too soft and fudgy.
